8 Games I'm *actually* playing on my Odin 3

September 1, 2026 (22 hours ago)View revision history

A common trend in most "retro handheld"/SBC gaming communities is this idea of buying a bunch of devices just to not use any of them to... play games. I myself have had to valiantly resist the urge to buy into this same problem, and landed on just owning one, the Odin 31.

Limited to one device, I have been forced to, y'know, play games, rather than browse Aliexpress for hours, so if you're stuck like I used to be on what exactly to play, here's some games that I've been enjoying:

Note: I use ROCKNIX on my Odin 3, not Android, so any info regarding compatibility/etc. pertains to only ROCKNIX.

The Games

1. Slay the Spire 2

I am a huge StS-head, and agree with Northernlion's sentiment that Slay the Spire is the best game ever made. Slay the Spire 2 is also fantastic, and being able to play it on the bed or out and about has been amazing.

To get it running requires some flags, which took a little while to get working, but the below launch options + Steam Linux Runtime 1.0 have worked great for me:

DOTNET_EnableWriteXorExecute=0 DOTNET_GCHeapHardLimit=0x400000000 %command% --rendering-driver vulkan

2. Midnight Club 3: DUB Edition Remix

This one is a game from my childhood, at least the PSP version was. Just an overall great street racing game that's super easy to pick up and put down without much notice.

The customization is deep enough that my pea brain is satisfied, and the music combined with the night driving creates a feeling that still holds up beautifully.

3. The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

I was a little late to this one and still haven't finished it, but Eden, the current surviving Yuzu fork, has been extremely capable of playing almost every Switch title for a while now. The Odin 3 rips this game without breaking much of a sweat too, which is nice.

4. Pokemon Unbound

Okay I know this one is a romhack but I'm counting it. This game is super cool, I find the story interesting and I've found it starts way less boring than how Pokemon games usually start. Another one that's easy to pick up where you left off, unless you take TOO long, which then you kinda forget what's going on and its hard to get back into.

5. Citizen Sleeper

A "sleeper" hit, if you will. This game is super neat, I've found it very engaging so far. It heats up the Odin 3 a little more than you'd think it would, but it's a small price to pay for such an awesome game.

6. Burnout 3/Revenge/Paradise

Each run great, pick your poison, they're all fantastic racing games.

7. Metaphor: ReFantazio

I've just started this one, and it's about the most demanding game I'm willing to throw at the Odin 3 before I start feeling like a public nuisance with the fan blowing. It runs pretty good at low settings, limited to 30 fps. I play at 1080p but bringing it down to 720, using LSFG, etc. might help as well.

8. Super Monkey Ball 2

Bonus Games
